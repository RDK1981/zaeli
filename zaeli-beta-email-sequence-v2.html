14-day tips

From: Rich (Zaeli) <hello@zaeli.ai>  ·  Subject: Two weeks in - a few things you might have missed
zaeli

Hi there,

Two weeks in — thanks for sticking with it. A quick note on three habits I see from the families getting the most out of Zaeli:

  1. Voice-first on the go. The mic on the home screen is faster than opening any other app for adding stuff. Try it walking to the car, at pickup, in the aisle at Woolies. It becomes muscle memory quickly.
  2. Read the brief on lockscreen, don't open the app. Around your morning brief time, a push lands with the full day's shape — meals, events, one nudge. Read it there and get on with your day. Zero-friction glance.
  3. Actually share with your partner. If they're still on Notes or the iCloud Calendar and you're on Zaeli, half the value is lost. More → Our Family → invite. Takes 90 seconds and unlocks the whole point.

Bonus: reminders can be personal (just you) OR shared (whole family). Say "remind me to grab milk" vs "remind us bins out tonight" — Zaeli picks up on the difference.

Halfway through the beta — how's it holding up? If there's a feature you wish existed, or one that's clunky enough to be worth mentioning, hit reply. I'm still adjusting a lot based on what real families tell me.

Talk soon,

Rich
