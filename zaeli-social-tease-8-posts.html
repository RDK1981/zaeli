💡 A few notes before you start posting

  • Recreate each mockup in Canva at 1080×1080 (Instagram square) — free templates. Use your exact colours (warm bg #FAF8F5, sky #A8D8F0 for the 'a' + 'i', coral #FF4545 for 'chaos'). Poppins ExtraBold for wordmark.
  • Use REAL screenshots where possible for Posts 4-7 (lockscreen brief, home tile, chat, sharing). Real UI reads more authentic than perfect mockups.
  • Post Tuesday or Wednesday 8-9am Brisbane — highest engagement window for parenting/family audience on both IG and FB.
  • Reply to every comment for the first 8 weeks. Even "❤️" and emoji-only. This teaches Instagram's algorithm that your posts drive conversation → more reach.
  • Track waitlist signups — check beta_signups in Supabase after each post to see which posts drive the most signups. Iterate accordingly.
  • Stories are optional but powerful — 2-3 Stories per week (mid-week) with behind-the-scenes / dev updates / real family moments can lift feed post reach 20-30%. Do only if sustainable.
  • Don't post 2× per week yet. Wait until you see genuine engagement on the first 3-4 posts. Then layer in Stories, then a second weekly post if you have the bandwidth.

🎯 Success metrics per post

  • Weeks 1-3 (atmospheric): Watch saves + shares, not follows. If the post is being saved, it's landing emotionally.
  • Weeks 4-7 (features): Watch DMs + comments asking "when can I try it?" — these are your hottest waitlist leads. Send them the signup link directly.
  • Week 8 (CTA): Watch waitlist signup count. If posts 1-7 did their job, you should see a signup surge in the 24h after posting.
