Zaeli v2 — Our Family redesign

Full overhaul post the v2 pivot: Kids Hub is hidden, Tutor add-on is hidden, kids now have the same core access as adults (Calendar, Shopping, Reminders, Chat) with Our Budget parent-toggled per-kid (default OFF). Our Family becomes the control surface for the household — roster, invites, access toggles — plus the entry to a member's own profile and account settings.

Design principles

  1. Kids are first-class — no more separate "Kids Hub" mode. A kid signs in and sees Home / Chat / Calendar / Shopping / Reminders just like an adult. Simpler mental model for the family.
  2. Budget is opt-in per kid — parents toggle it on the child's row. Default OFF. Kids never see Our Family management, ever (adults-only).
  3. "Add a member without a device" — dropped. Every member either has an invite pending or is a joined account. No shadow-members.
  4. Roles are visible and honest — every row shows Owner / Adult / Kid + join status. No hidden hierarchy.
  5. Tap-anywhere-on-row opens the member profile — where you edit name/colour/PIN/permissions, or remove them.
  6. Settings link kept top-of-mind — Our Family and Settings are siblings; the header hamburger jumps between the two. Owner-only sections in Settings (Subscription, Delete account) hidden for adult invitees and kids.

The roster + invite hub
Owner view is the full-fat screen. Adult invitees see a slightly slimmed variant (they can see the roster but not modify it — that's owner-only). Kids can't reach Our Family at all — the MoreSheet tile is hidden for kid accounts.
