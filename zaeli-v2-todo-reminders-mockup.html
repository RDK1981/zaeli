Section 2 · Reminders & to-dos sheet
Two tabs — Reminders / To-dos
Simplified from four tabs to two. Reminders = anything dated (time or day). To-dos = undated. Reminders tab groups by day-header (Today / Tomorrow / Fri 5 Aug). To-dos tab is flat with tier icons showing 🔒 personal / 📣 shared. Done items sit inline at bottom, greyed + strikethrough, auto-clear after 7 days (or user long-press to permanently delete).
