Rules of the game (memorise before posting)

  • Never link out. No zaeli.app, no App Store, no external URLs. Groups auto-flag them.
  • Never mention "Zaeli" in the post itself. Read the group rules — self-promotion = ban.
  • Post questions, not statements. "Drop a 🙋‍♀️ if..." beats "Here's what I think..."
  • Reply to every comment for the first 2 hours. Ask a follow-up. Warm the DM inbox.
  • Wait for DMs. When someone comments "OMG same" → 24h later slide into their DMs: "Hey, saw your comment on the group post. Genuinely so real. I've been quietly building something around this exact thing — want first dibs on the beta when it's ready?"
  • Don't join more than 3 groups per week. New members can't post immediately in most groups (7-14 day cool-down). Join early, wait.
  • Vary your post text. Don't copy-paste the same post into all 6 groups on the same day — algorithm flags duplicates.
  • Track DM→waitlist conversion. Warm DM → waitlist signup should hit 30-40%. Higher than any cold ad.
