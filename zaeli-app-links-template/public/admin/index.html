Revoke beta?

Sets beta_end_date to now — the family will lose beta access immediately. They'll fall back to whatever Stripe state applies (usually cancelled).

Delete family?

This permanently removes this family and everything they own: calendar, shopping, reminders, budget, chat history, memory, all family members. Their Supabase auth logins are also deleted so their emails free up for re-signup. Cannot be undone.
