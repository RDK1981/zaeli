Home — the front door

Everything that matters, at a glance.

Four tiles on Home cover 95% of the daily glue: Calendar · Shopping · Reminders · Budget. Each one leads with a plain-English headline you can read in a heartbeat.

  • ✓Big-text headlines. "2 events on today." · "4 items to grab." · "1 due at 3 pm." · "On track for the month." No decoding.
  • ✓Type or tap the mic in any tile. "Milk, bread, coffee" — added. Say "Duke's swim next Thursday 4pm" — booked.
  • ✓Tap any tile to expand. Full calendar, full list, everything. Sheet pops over Home — you never lose your place.
  • ✓Chat is one swipe left. For anything that needs a proper conversation, or a photo, or a brain-dump.

Morning + evening brief

Zaeli reads your day to you before you open the app.

Every morning and evening at the time you pick, Zaeli lands a proper brief on your lockscreen. Not "you have new notifications". The actual brief — what's on today, what needs doing, what's coming.

  • ✓Real content in the body. A paragraph you can read from the lockscreen without unlocking your phone.
  • ✓Aware of your family's actual day. Pulls from today's calendar, tomorrow's calendar, your shopping list, your open reminders.
  • ✓Warm, not naggy. If nothing needs doing, she'll tell you that too. No fake urgency.
  • ✓Pick your times. 7am morning, 5pm evening, or whenever fits your rhythm.
Reminders — the 4th pillar

Family-shared. Notifications just for you.

A reminder should nudge the person who set it — not the whole family. But the whole family should be able to see the list. Zaeli's reminders finally get this right.

  • ✓Shared visibility. Anna can see the reminders Rich set. Rich can see Anna's. One family view.
  • ✓Creator-only push. Your phone buzzes for the reminders you set. Nobody else's.
  • ✓Timed, date-only, or someday. "3pm today" fires a push. "Sometime Friday" shows on Friday. "Someday" waits patiently.
  • ✓Recurring built in. Every Monday, weekdays only, fortnightly, monthly — Zaeli generates the whole series ahead of time.
  • ✓Voice, text, or via Zaeli. Type it, dictate it, or say it in chat — "remind me to call the plumber Friday 3pm."

Chat — for everything the tile isn't enough for

Talk to her like a switched-on friend.

Home covers the quick adds. Chat covers everything else — the natural-language stuff you'd say to a partner, the photos you'd forward to the fridge, the "what have we got Saturday?"

  • ✓Voice, text, or photo. Snap a school newsletter — she reads it and files every event.
  • ✓One question, one answer. "Did we buy grapes?" "Yes — last Tuesday." "What's on Saturday?" "Anna's parents lunch at 1pm."
  • ✓Notify chip on every add. One tap and the whole family knows.
  • ✓She remembers. Poppy plays soccer. Gab's got peanut allergies. Rich's brother's in Adelaide. Zaeli learns and uses it.
The productivity trio

Calendar. Shopping. Budget. Reminders.
Done properly.

📅

Calendar

One family calendar, colour-coded per person. Add by voice, tap, or photo. Notify the whole family with one chip after every add. Recurring events understand "every second Wednesday" without you having to explain twice.

🛒

Shopping

Type in the tile — faster than iPhone Notes. Or dictate. Or snap a receipt — Zaeli reads it, ticks off what you bought, and notes the last-bought date. Pantry never blocks an add (real families know pantries aren't 100%).

⏰

Reminders

Family-shared list. Creator-only push. Timed, date-only, or someday. Recurring built in. Voice, text, or ask Zaeli — "remind me to call the plumber Friday 3pm" — done.

💰

Budget

A planner, not a tracker. Fixed and variable expenses as a flat list — no messy category-of-category nesting. Set targets, track savings goals. Zaeli reads statements you upload and suggests the numbers.
