Zaeli v2 — Shopping sheet redesign

Round B redesign addressing Rich's 5 complaints. Same tab surface, but visually pared back to match the Reminders & To-dos sheet + Home bento vibe. Cost per change is low; combined they lift the sheet from "acceptable" to "on-brand".

What we're changing (and why)

  1. Kill search bar — 7-item lists don't need search; row-tap-to-edit already covers the "find + change" flow.
  2. Kill List / Aisle toggle — aisle-mode was a v1 exploration nobody uses. Category grouping stays under the hood if we need it later.
  3. Slimmer rows — 60px → 44px min height. Fits ~2 more rows on screen without keyboard, easier to scan.
  4. Scroll arrows repositioned top-right (inside the body area, above the bar) — used to overlap the add pill.
  5. Redesign the bottom pill — matches the Reminders sheet's `[mic | text | camera | send]` unified pill. Lavender tinted to match Shopping accent. Same 44×44 buttons, same 24px radius. Zaeli button still there but as a chip inside the pill or a separate row above.
