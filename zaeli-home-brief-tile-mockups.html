My pick Variation B — Compact opener

Best balance of Zaeli-voice-moment vs Home-tile-hierarchy. The opening line ("Monday morning, Rich — soccer 4pm for Gab...") is the payoff sentence anyway — most briefs' first line contains 80% of the actionable info. The "Read full brief →" gives escape hatch for users who want more without punishing users who don't.

Why not A: takes too much space above the fold. Home's job is glance. Full-brief-visible pushes Calendar tile down — users have to scroll to see today's events. Wrong hierarchy.

Why not C: loses Zaeli's warmth. She reads like a system notification, not a friend. The peach/lavender tint helps but truncated to one micro-line feels alerty. The whole reason we keep this tile is to preserve the personality moment — C sacrifices that for space.

Bonus for B: if user taps to expand, we can open a sheet with the full brief text + chips (Chat with me / Got it). Expanded sheet = premium moment. Compact tile = ambient presence. Best of both.

Empty state (before brief-scheduler fires for the day): tile hides entirely. Cleaner than showing "Waiting for morning brief..." or a placeholder — nothing to see, nothing rendered. Once the server generates the brief and upserts to zaeli_briefs, the tile appears on next Home refresh (which cache-then-refresh handles).

Empty state (nothing pressing): if the brief text is genuinely "clear day, nothing pressing", the tile still shows — that's a valuable message ("you can breathe today"). Don't hide the tile just because there are no events. Zaeli's job is competent reassurance, not just information.

Position on Home: above Calendar tile. First thing user sees when they open Home. Preserves the "brief is the daily hello" ritual.
