01 — Principles

The four rules that drive every decision.

These are the constraints. Every screen, every tap, every AI call gets measured against them. When something doesn't earn its place, it goes away.

🎯 Glance-first, not navigate-first

Users open a family app to check what's happening, not to travel to a section. The Home screen shows everything relevant in one view. Drill in only when you need more.

⚡ Speed beats AI for repeated actions

If iPhone Notes is faster than us for the daily add-a-thing task, we lose the user. Inline quick-add fields + voice FAB give parity or better on every atomic action.

🧠 AI shines on fuzzy + cross-cutting

"Plan Poppy's birthday", "cancel Friday's meal and rebudget", "add these 8 events from a school newsletter". Chat and vision earn their place here.

🗂 Hide, don't delete

Screens Anna and Rich don't use get moved to app/_hidden/ (Expo Router ignores underscore-prefixed routes). Code stays in git, ready to revive if the market signals demand later.

What's on this screen

Five tiles. That's it. Every one earns its place.

  • Brief tile — Zaeli's morning/evening brief lives on Home, not tucked in chat. Peach for morning, lavender for evening. Two chips: "Open chat" (expands into the conversation) or "Got it" (dismisses for the day).
  • Calendar tile — today's events, colour-coded avatars. Quick-add field. Tap the tile to open the full Calendar sheet.
  • Shopping tile — the Anna fix. Live count, top items, and the always-visible quick-add row at the bottom. Type + Enter = added. No AI in the fast path.
  • Bento pair — Weather + Budget balance. Budget tile shows month-so-far. Tap for full breakdown.
  • FAB (bottom-right) — coral mic. Tap from anywhere in the app. Voice command → Sonnet → tool call → done. Details in section 05.
Design promise: The most common action a parent takes in a day — "add milk to shopping" — happens in less than 2 seconds either by tapping the quick-add row and typing, or by tapping the FAB and speaking.

Speed parity with iPhone Notes

Before: Open Zaeli → tap chat → type "add butter to shopping" → wait for Sonnet routing → wait for tool call → confirmation. 6–10 seconds per item.

Now: Open Zaeli → Shopping tile is right there → tap the pinned add row → type "butter" → Enter. 2–3 seconds per item. Faster than Notes.

  • Add row pinned at top — always visible, always focused when you tap in.
  • No AI in the write path — direct Supabase insert. Zero latency beyond the network.
  • Category auto-detected from local heuristics (bakery/fruit/dairy/etc). Not a Sonnet call.
  • Voice still works via FAB — "add butter and eggs and yeast" batches through Sonnet if you want multi-item.
  • Pantry-aware chip retained — "you bought this 3 days ago, still add it?" — but on demand, not blocking every add.
What changes structurally: The old chat-driven add_shopping_item tool stays available for voice/chat. But the primary path — Anna's daily add — is now the pinned form. AI is optional, not required.

What Chat sheet is for

Fuzzy things Sonnet is uniquely good at. Cross-module reasoning, planning, brain dump, photo upload, brief interaction.

  • Brief lives here too — the same brief that shows as a Dashboard tile expands here when you tap "Open chat".
  • Multi-tool actions — "We're eating out Friday, cancel that meal and add $80 to the eating out budget" — one Zaeli message, multiple tools called across meals + budget.
  • Photo brain-dump — take a photo of the school newsletter, Zaeli reads and files 8 events.
  • Recipe → shopping — "add ingredients for tonight's bolognese to shopping" — chat pulls recipe context and adds only what's missing from the pantry.
  • The companion moments — "help me plan Poppy's birthday", "we've had a rough week, remind me what's going well". Zaeli's personality earns its place here.

Entry points: Chat tile on Dashboard · "Open chat" chip on brief · Sonnet-decides-when a voice command needs conversation (e.g. FAB voice "help me plan Poppy's birthday" opens the chat with the question pre-loaded).

09 — The brief, reimagined

From weather report → friend who's paying attention.

Anna's blunt verdict: today's brief is annoying noise. Says "clear day ahead" when the calendar isn't clear. Doesn't recall past events. Doesn't nudge things worth nudging. Just repeats what she can already see. We're rebuilding what a Zaeli brief actually is.

Today (annoying)
☀ MORNING
Thursday morning, Rich — clear day ahead ☀️

Sometimes that's the whole gift.
WHAT'S WRONG
  • ❌ Says "clear day" when there ARE events later
  • ❌ Zero recall of yesterday or the past week
  • ❌ No reminders surfaced
  • ❌ No observations Zaeli should be earning
  • ❌ Reads like a weather app, not a friend
v2 (valuable)
☀ MORNING
Thursday morning, Rich — hope Poppy's soccer went well last night 🌱

School pickup at 2, Poppy's dance at 3:30 today. One thing: pack Gab's library bag before you leave 🎒

Noticed the plumber's followed up twice this week — worth a reply?
WHY THIS WORKS
  • ✅ Opens with past-recall — "hope soccer went well" — friend-mode
  • ✅ Names specific events, doesn't invent quiet
  • ✅ Surfaces the reminder Zaeli's been holding for this day
  • ✅ Zaeli Noticed observation earns its place
  • ✅ Reads like someone paying attention

The four data inputs Zaeli needs to write a good brief

Right now buildBriefContext gives Sonnet: today's events, tomorrow's events, tonight's meal, shopping count, open tasks, weather, family names. It's missing the ingredients that make a brief worth reading.

1️⃣ Past-recall data New

Query events where date >= today - 3 and date < today. Give Sonnet the last 3 days' events so she can open with "hope X went well" or "how did Y go?" — when it's genuinely worth mentioning.

2️⃣ Today's reminders New

Query reminders table where remind_at::date = today. Reminders that live nowhere else finally get surfaced. Zaeli's brief becomes the one place they land.

3️⃣ Family insights Wire up existing

The family_insights table has Zaeli's learned observations (Session 23 wired the extraction — Session 24 warned about hallucination). Inject high-confidence insights into brief context, framed as "notice-worthy" not "reference-worthy".

4️⃣ Absolute event-visibility rule Prompt fix

If TODAY EVENTS has anything, Zaeli is BANNED from saying "clear day / quiet day / nothing on / breathing room". Session 26's invisible-domain rule works in reverse: if events exist, they must be acknowledged. No exceptions.

Persona shift: Zaeli isn't a Google Calendar summary. She's the friend who remembered Poppy had soccer last night, saw the calendar for today, checked what you asked her to remember, and noticed the plumber's still waiting on a reply. That's what a family app pays for — not a weather forecast of your own calendar.
Cost implication: Adding past-events + reminders + insights adds ~500-1000 input tokens per brief. With prompt caching (Session 21) this costs maybe ~A$0.001 extra per brief. Two briefs a day × 30 days = ~A$0.06/family/mo. Negligible for the value.

Implementation: Prompt rewrite in lib/brief-generator.ts + three additions to buildBriefContext in index.tsx. No new tables. No new UI. Just a smarter brief. Should be Phase 04-05 material — after Bento lands so we can see it working on the tile.
